4. Core Takeaway

Pressure is the amount of force exerted over a given unit of area.

  • Increase Force (same area) $\rightarrow$ Pressure Increases
  • Increase Area (same force) $\rightarrow$ Pressure Decreases

Formula: Pressure (Pa) = Force (N) ÷ Area (m²)
